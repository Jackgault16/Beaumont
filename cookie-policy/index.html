Cookie Policy

How Beaumont Archives uses essential and analytics cookies.

Beaumont Archives uses essential site functionality to operate forms, navigation and catalogue features.

With consent, Beaumont Archives also uses Google Analytics cookies to understand website performance and visitor traffic. This helps identify which pages are useful, how visitors move through the site, and where the website can be improved.

Analytics cookies are non-essential. Visitors can accept analytics cookies or reject non-essential cookies using the cookie banner shown on the website.

If non-essential cookies are rejected, Beaumont Archives will continue to provide the main website functionality without enabling analytics storage.

Cookie preferences may be changed by clearing browser site data for beaumontarchives.co.uk and revisiting the website.
