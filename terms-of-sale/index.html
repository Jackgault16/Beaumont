Buying & Policies

Terms of Sale

Beaumont Archives operates as an enquiry-led dealer.

All items are offered subject to availability and prior sale.

Descriptions are prepared in good faith using available bibliographic, historical and condition information. Beaumont Archives endeavours to note significant faults, restoration, incompleteness, inscriptions, provenance and other relevant details where known.

Prices may be listed directly or supplied on request. For enquiry-led purchases, the final price, shipping cost and payment details will be confirmed before sale.

Reproduction prints are sold as modern reproductions and not as original maps or documents. Where a listing concerns a reproduction, this must be clearly stated in the item description.

Antiquarian and archive material may show age-related wear, including toning, foxing, rubbing, previous ownership marks, repairs, folds, tears or other signs of use. Such characteristics are part of the nature of historic material unless otherwise stated.

Ownership of an item transfers only once payment has been received in full.

Beaumont Archives does not provide formal insurance or probate valuations unless expressly agreed in writing. Any pricing guidance, estimate or catalogue note is for general sale and research purposes only.
