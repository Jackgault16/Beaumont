Collection Services

Research, sourcing and collection guidance

Beaumont offers research-based collection guidance, market observations, provenance research and sourcing support for collectors, families and institutions.

  • Collection reviews
  • Provenance research
  • Historical research
  • Material sourcing
  • Collection development
  • Acquisition guidance
  • Consignment advice
  • Object identification

Beaumont provides research and market guidance based upon available historical and market information. Formal probate, insurance and regulated valuation services should be sought through appropriately qualified professionals where required.

Collection Development & Sourcing

Looking For Something Specific?

Beaumont assists collectors, researchers and institutions in locating books, maps, manuscripts and historical material that may not currently appear within our catalogue.

Through specialist research, dealer networks, auction monitoring and collection contacts, Beaumont can help identify and source material aligned to a client's collecting interests.

Whether searching for a regimental history, a battlefield map, a rare travel narrative or a specific historical document, enquiries are welcomed.

Specialist Interests

Rare Books

Military History
