Fine Books, Maps & Historical Objects

BEAUMONT

Beaumont specialises in rare books, historical maps, manuscripts and historical objects.

Areas of particular interest include military history, exploration, travel, local history, cartography and historical archives.

Services include collection reviews, provenance research, collection development, acquisition advice, material sourcing and consignment guidance.

Every item is researched, catalogued and presented with attention to historical context, rarity, condition and provenance.

Beaumont provides research-based guidance and market observations. Formal probate, insurance and regulated valuation services should be obtained from appropriately qualified professionals where required.

Specialist Areas

What we specialise in

Fine & Rare Books

First editions, important historical works, association copies and volumes with strong collecting merit.

Military History

Campaign histories, regimental works, memoirs, battlefield studies and material connected to the First and Second World Wars.

Maps & Cartography

Printed maps, trench maps, survey material, travel maps and cartographic records with historical significance.

Manuscripts

Letters, journals, signed material, working papers and manuscript items with clear context and provenance.

Historical Documents

Archival records, official papers, ephemera and documents connected to people, places and events.

Travel & Exploration

Exploration narratives, voyages, expedition accounts, travel literature and geographic works.

Historical Objects

Carefully selected objects where provenance, condition and historical meaning support serious collecting.

Private Collections

Assessment, cataloguing, placement and sale advice for private libraries, estates and specialist collections.
